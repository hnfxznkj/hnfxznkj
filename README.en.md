## What the company does

The company designs, manufactures, and installs outdoor solar power systems for new-energy and IoT power needs. The core setting is an outdoor site with no utility power, a harsh environment, and a difficult power supply.

It offers customized off-grid PV kits that combine storage, control, and protection. The aim is continuous power where equipment is scattered, trenching is expensive, and the site must run unattended: hydrology stations, emergency terminals, and IoT sensors.

Points stated on the website:

- Complete off-grid PV kits
- Battery thermal enclosure
- Outdoor sites with no utility power
- Storage, control, and protection in one system
- Survey through operation and maintenance

The published principle is “professional, stable, innovative.” The stated aim is to be a trusted outdoor solar-power solution provider.

## Problems addressed

Owners of water, geohazard, emergency, security, high-standard farmland, and urban-network projects repeatedly face three constraints:

1. **No grid, hard to cable.** Stations, loudspeakers, and cameras sit in fields, on slopes, on ridgelines, or along corridors. Trenching takes time and money.
2. **Harsh duty.** Heat, cold, rain, snow, dust, and day–night temperature swings require continuous, stable power.
3. **It must stay on, often unattended.** Precision monitoring loads should not drop casually. The site needs charge–discharge control and a kit that can actually be installed.

Schemes are sized to the load and the local sun. They are meant for job sites, not lab demos. There is no public price list; pricing is on request.
